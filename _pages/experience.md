### Team Learning for Better Scientific Software (TL4BSSw)
{: #tl4bssw data-nav-label="TL4BSSw"}

<p class="exp-entry__role"><strong>Project Contributor</strong> · DOE, NNSA, and NSF funded · 2024 – present</p>

<p class="exp-entry__outcome">Co-author of a team learning framework for interdisciplinary scientific software teams, now under revision at <em>IEEE Computing in Science and Engineering</em>.</p>

<img src="/images/experience/tl4bssw-workshop-2024.jpg" alt="The TL4BSSw workshop team at UCF's Institute for Simulation and Training, November 2024" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="635">

- Part of a 12-person team of researchers from universities and national laboratories, building an open knowledge base and practical tools that help interdisciplinary teams learn while they build scientific software.
- Wrote a position paper for the November 2024 workshop at UCF's Institute for Simulation and Training.

<p class="exp-entry__links"><a href="https://tl4bssw.github.io/">Visit the workshop site</a></p>

### Externalized and Extended Cognition: Cognitive Offloading for Human-Machine Teaming
{: #lockheed data-nav-label="Lockheed Martin"}

<p class="exp-entry__role"><strong>Research Lead</strong> · Lockheed Martin · 2023 – 2026</p>

<p class="exp-entry__outcome">Three design recommendations for managing pilot workload, adopted into Lockheed's engineering design process.</p>

<img src="/images/experience/lockheed-cognitive-unit-merged.png" alt="Environment, system, and pilot treated as one cognitive unit" loading="lazy" width="1132" height="562">

- Built a design framework on an existing cognitive task analysis of the Future Vertical Lift environment, separating cognitive offloading from scaffolding as two independent levers for pilot workload.
- Designed the experiments to test each recommendation, after scoping the problem in working sessions with Lockheed and partner engineering teams.
- Findings informed a UI overhaul of an active operations monitoring and information management system.

<p class="exp-entry__links"><a href="/case-studies/lockheed/">Read the <span class="sr-only">Lockheed Martin </span>case study</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1177/10711813241275506">HFES 2024 paper</a></p>

</div>

### Cognition in Support of Future Space Missions
{: #minerva data-nav-label="Minerva"}

<p class="exp-entry__role"><strong>Research Staff, Experiment Coordinator</strong> · U.S. DoD Minerva Research Initiative, with NASA · June 2023 – Fall 2025</p>

<p class="exp-entry__outcome">A multi-university research team studying how teams think, solve problems, and coordinate, and building unobtrusive, multi-sensor measures of individual and team cognitive states for long-duration space missions.</p>

<img src="/images/experience/minerva-team-study.png" alt="Teams work through collaborative problem-solving tasks while the studies capture communication, behavior, self-report, task performance, physiological, and environmental data, all feeding into measures of team cognition" loading="lazy" width="2264" height="1120">

- Coordinated the team's experiments, including participant scheduling, IRB protocols, and work with partner labs at other universities.
- Built and ran the behavioral coding and transcription training that turned session recordings into usable data.
- Assisted with assembling the team's sensor suite, and contributed to its workflow and environment design recommendations for missions with human and AI (onboard system) team members.

<p class="exp-entry__note"><em>The project wound down in 2025, after the Department of Defense ended the Minerva Research Initiative and terminated its research grants.</em></p>

### Quality Assurance Program and Epic EHR Launch
{: #orlando-health data-nav-label="Orlando Health"}

<p class="exp-entry__role"><strong>Supervisor, Process Improvement Coordinator</strong> · Orlando Health Imaging Centers · 2020 – 2022</p>

<p class="exp-entry__outcome">60% fewer errors and 40% higher departmental productivity in under six months.</p>

- Paired operational data analysis with task analysis, field observation, a diary study that ran for over a month, staff surveys, and focus groups, to find where errors landed and why they happened.
- Defined performance metrics and led the process redesign, including standardized workflows with written guides, staff retraining, staffing and schedule changes, and ongoing audits.
- Managed training, built the launch reference resources, and ran usability testing on the EHR when the organization moved to Epic.
