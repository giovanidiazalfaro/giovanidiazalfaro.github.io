## The problem

Long-duration space missions need a way to monitor the cognitive state of individuals and of the team as a whole. Our team set out to develop unobtrusive measures of team functioning. The studies put teams through collaborative problem-solving tasks and captured how they communicated, coordinated, and performed, alongside physiological and environmental sensor data.

## Running the studies

As Experiment Coordinator I handled the IRB protocols, screened, recruited, and scheduled participants, trained and calibrated research assistants so sessions ran the same way every time, and coordinated with partner labs at other universities.

## What the team produced, and my part

I helped develop the team's unobtrusive multi-sensor measures of individual and team cognitive states, and contributed to its workflow and environment design recommendations for long-duration missions with human and AI (onboard system) team members.

## What carries over

The method is the part that travels. This project paired what sensors record with what people say and do, which is the same pairing of behavioral data and direct research that product teams rely on.
